Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Trendlo. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our AI-powered social media management application.

Trendlo is a mobile application that helps users create, schedule, and post AI-generated content across multiple social media platforms including X (Twitter), Telegram, Threads, Bluesky, Discord, Reddit, Mastodon, LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Email address (for authentication)
  • User ID and authentication tokens
  • Account creation and last login dates
  • Premium subscription status

2.2 Social Media Credentials

To enable posting to your social media accounts, the app requires credentials for the platforms you connect. These credentials are stored locally on your device using encrypted secure storage and are never sent to or stored on our servers.

Supported platforms include: X (Twitter), Telegram, Threads, Bluesky, Discord, Reddit, Mastodon, LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram.

2.3 Content Data

  • Post content you create or generate
  • Images you upload (up to 4 per post)
  • Scheduled posts and posting history
  • AI generation prompts and preferences
  • Selected writing styles and languages

2.4 Usage Data

  • App usage statistics and analytics
  • AI model preferences (Groq, Cerebras, Mistral)
  • Feature usage patterns
  • Error logs and crash reports
  • Device information (OS version, app version)

2.5 Settings and Preferences

  • Language preferences (app and content)
  • Theme settings
  • Enabled platforms
  • Auto-poster configurations
  • Notification preferences

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Core Functionality

  • Authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • Generate AI-powered content using your selected providers
  • Post content to your connected social media platforms
  • Schedule and automate posts according to your preferences
  • Store and retrieve your post history
  • Sync your settings across devices

3.2 Service Improvement

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features
  • Monitor and fix technical issues
  • Develop new features based on user needs
  • Optimize AI content generation quality

3.3 Communication

  • Send important service updates
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Notify you about premium features and subscriptions

4. Third-Party Services

Trendlo integrates with several third-party services to provide its functionality:

4.1 Authentication and Database

  • Supabase: Handles user authentication, stores user data, settings, and post history. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

4.2 Backend Infrastructure

  • Cloudflare Workers: Processes API requests, manages OAuth flows, and serves as our backend infrastructure.

4.3 AI Content Generation

  • Groq: Provides AI models (Llama 3.1 8B, Llama 3.3 70B) for content generation
  • Cerebras: Provides AI models (Llama 3.1 8B, GPT-OSS 120B) for content generation
  • Mistral AI: Provides AI models (Mistral Small, Large, Codestral) for content generation

Your prompts and generated content are sent to these services for processing. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

4.4 Social Media Platforms

When you connect and post to social media platforms, you are subject to their terms of service and privacy policies:

  • X (Twitter)
  • Telegram
  • Meta Threads
  • Bluesky
  • Discord
  • Reddit
  • Mastodon
  • LinkedIn
  • Facebook
  • Instagram

4.5 Error Reporting

  • We may use error reporting tools to collect crash data and help us fix bugs and improve stability. No personal data is included in error reports.

4.6 Premium Subscriptions

  • RevenueCat: Manages premium subscriptions and in-app purchases. Payment information is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Storage Location

Account data, settings, and post history are stored in Supabase databases with encryption at rest and in transit. Social media credentials are stored locally on your device only using encrypted secure storage and are never uploaded to our servers.

5.2 Security Measures

  • HTTPS encryption for data transmission
  • Secure token-based authentication via Supabase
  • On-device encrypted storage for social media credentials
  • Ongoing security updates

5.3 Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active
  • Post history: Retained until you delete it or close your account
  • Usage analytics: Retained for 12 months
  • Error logs: Retained for 90 days

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Control

  • View and edit your account information
  • Manage connected social media accounts
  • Delete your post history
  • Disconnect platforms at any time

6.2 Account Deletion

You can request account deletion by contacting us at support@trendlo.app. Upon deletion:

  • Your account and personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Post history and generated content will be removed
  • Social media credentials will be securely erased
  • Some data may be retained for legal compliance (e.g., transaction records)

6.3 Marketing Communications

You can opt out of promotional emails at any time. Service-related communications cannot be opted out of while your account is active.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

7.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

7.2 Service Providers

We share data with service providers only as necessary to operate our service (Supabase, Cloudflare, AI providers, etc.).

7.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and safety.

7.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred to the new entity.

8. Children's Privacy

Trendlo is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this privacy policy.

10. Cookies and Tracking

Trendlo uses minimal tracking technologies:

  • Authentication tokens to maintain your session
  • Local storage for app preferences and settings
  • Analytics to understand app usage patterns

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or trackers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email. Continued use of Trendlo after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us:

Email: support@trendlo.app

We will respond to your inquiry within 7 business days.

Your Privacy Matters: We are committed to transparency and protecting your data. If you have concerns about how your information is handled, please reach out to us directly.
